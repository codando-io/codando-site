Making public data easier to understand.

Prumo is a civic transparency platform for Brazilian electoral data. It starts from the campaign finance records published by Brazil’s Superior Electoral Court (TSE) and turns the raw open-data files into something anyone can explore: where each campaign’s money came from, and where it went.

It covers the 2026 general elections, while accounts are still being filed, and the 2022 and 2018 elections.

Traceable by design

Every important number links to its source. Official values, Prumo’s own calculations, and statistical patterns are always labeled apart, and patterns are described, never presented as evidence of wrongdoing.

Prumo is independent and has no affiliation with the TSE, political parties, or candidates. Parties never get a color.
