What we’re building

Where the money in Brazil’s election campaigns came from, and where it went.

&ngsp;Prumo covers the 2026, 2022, and 2018 general elections.

Prumo turns the campaign finance data published by Brazil’s Superior Electoral Court (TSE) into something anyone can explore: candidate profiles, money flows, donors and suppliers, and the connections between them.

    Independent and neutral

    Prumo has no affiliation with the TSE, political parties, or candidates. Parties never get a color, and patterns in the data are described, never presented as evidence of wrongdoing.
